# Ironbreaker design review notes

## Cooldown

Impenetrable uses a 120-second cooldown starting on activation to address the permanent uptime observed in play. This preserves skill-based duration while leaving a substantial gap between uses. 

## Gromril Armor and Rune Weapons

While Impenetrable is active, each enemy melee hit you take or block with your shield adds one stack, up to three. Ranged attacks do not count. When Impenetrable ends, Gromril Armor gives 5% Physical Resistance per stack, and Rune Weapons gives 5% Physical damage per stack. Each bonus lasts 10 seconds and caps at 15%. You only receive the bonus for a keystone you have selected. You get rewarded for being a tank.

## Hybrid career

Ironbreaker is a hybrid career built around close-range melee and shooting—not a pure melee or pure ranged career. The reload and Gunpowder bonuses on Gromril Armor, Rune Weapons, and Irondrakes are intentional. Selecting all three gives a combined 75% reload-speed bonus and adds 0.06 seconds of Impenetrable duration per Gunpowder point.

## Nest Cleansing

The intended protection covers nonmagical siege and explosive weapons: catapults, trebuchets, cannons, grenades, Trollhammers, and grenadier weapons. Magical blasts are excluded from this additional protection; ordinary fire resistance remains separate.

## Shieldwall

Shieldwall intentionally shares Impenetrable with all nearby allies, regardless of race or troop type.
