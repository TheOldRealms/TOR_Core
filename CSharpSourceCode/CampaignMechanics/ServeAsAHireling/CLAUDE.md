# CampaignMechanics/ServeAsAHireling

Lets the player take service as a hireling under an AI lord/faction instead of leading
their own party.

- **`ServeAsAHirelingCampaignBehavior`** (`: CampaignBehaviorBase`) — entry/exit points and
  state management for the activity.
- **`ServeAsAHirelingActivities`** — the day-to-day activities/tasks performed while serving.
- **`ServeAsAHirelingHelpers`** (static) — shared query/eligibility helpers.
