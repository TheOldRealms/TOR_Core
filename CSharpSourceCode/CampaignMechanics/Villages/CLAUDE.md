# CampaignMechanics/Villages

- **`TORVillageTypes`** (`: DefaultVillageTypes`) — TOR's replacement/extension of the
  vanilla village-type list (per-culture village flavors matching Warhammer factions).
- **`TORTownManagementVillageItemVM`** — view-model for a village row in the town
  management screen, extended for TOR's village data.
