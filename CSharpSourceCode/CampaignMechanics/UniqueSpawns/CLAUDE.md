# CampaignMechanics/UniqueSpawns

Named unique heroes/monsters spawned into the campaign world (lore characters/creatures
rather than generic troops).

- **`UniqueSpawnCampaignBehavior`** (`: CampaignBehaviorBase`) — generic framework for
  spawning and tracking unique named entities.
- **`UniqueSpawnPartyComponent`** (`: WarPartyComponent`) — party component marking a
  unique spawn's war party.
- **`OrionCampaignBehavior`** — a specific named unique spawn (Orion, the Wood Elf King in
  the Wood — a Warhammer lore character), its own dedicated behavior.
