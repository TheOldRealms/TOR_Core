# CampaignMechanics/Companions

- **`TORCompanionsCampaignBehavior`** (`: CampaignBehaviorBase`) — custom companion
  recruitment/management rules (companion pools likely skewed by culture/race, matching
  Warhammer factions).
- **`TORAICompanionCampaignBehavior`** — AI-side companion behavior (how AI lords use/hire
  companions).
