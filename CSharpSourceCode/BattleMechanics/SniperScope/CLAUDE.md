# BattleMechanics/SniperScope

**`SniperScope`** (`: ICrosshair`) — a zoom/scope-style aiming reticle for long-range
weapons (e.g. Empire long rifles from `BattleMechanics/Firearms`), separate from the
`AbilitySystem/CrossHairs` family since it's a weapon-aiming aid rather than an
ability-targeting reticle.
