# BattleMechanics/Dismemberment

**`DismembermentMissionLogic`** (`: MissionLogic`) — gore mechanic: on a sufficiently heavy
killing blow, detaches/replaces limb meshes on the corpse instead of (or alongside) the
normal ragdoll, matching Warhammer's more brutal combat tone. Added in
`SubModule.OnMissionBehaviorInitialize`.
